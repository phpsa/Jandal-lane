A New Name, Same Great Spot

Jandal Lane Cafe

Nestled in the heart of New Brighton, Jandal Lane Cafe is a vibrant, community-loved cafe known for its scrumptious breakfasts — think bacon benny, hearty egg dishes and French toast — alongside great coffee. Our cheerful, attentive team and relaxed, dog-friendly atmosphere make every visit feel like coming home, whether it's a busy Saturday morning or a quiet weekday break.

Popular Items

Americano

A double shot of espresso lengthened with hot water for a smooth, bold cup with a lighter body than a flat white — clean and classic.

Cappuccino

Espresso topped with steamed milk and a generous layer of silky microfoam — rich, balanced and full of flavour.

Mocha

Espresso and steamed milk meet real chocolate for a decadent, coffee-forward treat with a sweet edge.

Blueberry Muffins

Baked fresh daily and packed with juicy blueberries — soft, moist and just sweet enough with your morning coffee.

Cheese Scones

Warm, savoury scones loaded with cheese and baked until golden — perfect on their own or split with a little butter.

Hangover Breakfast

Our hearty fix-it-all plate — bacon, eggs, hash browns and all the trimmings to get you back on your feet.

New Products

Bacon & Egg Croissant

Protein Shakes

Fresh Baked Biscuits

A rotating selection of biscuits baked in-house every morning — simple, buttery and best with a hot coffee.
